# KathanaBot 1.0.98

## What changed

- **Lite AutoPots' "Show HP/MP Overlay" now jumps to a zoomed live preview.** Clicking it also switches to the Vision tab, selects the `hp_bar` row in Calibration Regions, and turns on Live + Zoom Selected Region, so the tiny HP/MP bars are shown magnified right away instead of only the small drag-overlay on the actual game window. The same jump-to-zoomed-preview behavior now also backs the "Calibrate Buff Area" button.

## Recent change history - last 5

1. **Lite AutoPots' "Show HP/MP Overlay" jumps to a zoomed Vision tab preview** of hp_bar automatically, making the bars much easier to see while aligning the overlay.
2. **Auto Resurrect/Auto Accept Party-Ress reliability fixes**, an overlay-vs-OCR capture conflict fix, split Auto Accept Party/Ress into two buttons, and removed Retarget Now (E).
3. **Fixed Auto Relaunch's post-launch clicks crashing** with an overflow error on multi-monitor setups.
4. **Buff Watch**: auto-recast a buff when its icon disappears from a shared, calibrated Buff Area, with a categorized icon library/picker. Also: Hold to Show Game Window now works reliably across multiple monitors.
5. **Much more complete Fixed AutoPots help (EN/ES/FIL)** in the Lite tab, replacing the old terse EN/ES-only blurb with a full step-by-step walkthrough.
